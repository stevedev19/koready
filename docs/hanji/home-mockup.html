Needs your decision

  • Exchange "Updated HH:MM": the exchange API returns only the rate date, not a time. Showing a time means adding a fetch timestamp to /api/exchange, which is a small data change. The other option is to keep "As of Oct 1" on that card. The mockup shows the time version. Weather and Air already return observedAt, so they need no change.
  • New text: tips, Korean labels, "Updated", the converter labels and the emergency sheet are new strings. They'd go in strings.ts. Existing text stays as it is.
  • Rain tip threshold: it shows at a rain chance of 60% or more. The mask tip shows for Moderate (info blue, "if you're sensitive") and Bad or Very bad (amber).
  • Tab labels are already 13px, which meets your "at least 12px", so they stay 13px.
  • 112 source: helpLines.ts currently cites a voice-phishing article for 112. I'd switch it to the korea.net article (2024-03-19) that confirms 24h English interpretation.

Color rules applied

  • Indigo: active tab (solid), primary buttons, the slang card and the converter result.
  • Jade: card icons, glance icons, and Korean labels (jade-text). There's no jade on any result or status.
  • Red: only the emergency pill icon, the 119/112 sheet and "Likely scam". Air quality never turns red. Very bad is amber.
  • The Safety tab uses neutral cards, a high-contrast tool switch, 1.5px field borders, and keeps red and amber for results only.
