Korea Survival Kit · Design proposal v2 (modern)

Calm, clear, modern.

Revised for a modern mobile look, in the style of today's Korean fintech and super-apps: big bold titles, a soft grey canvas with white cards, rounded icon tiles, full-width bottom actions, bottom sheets and a floating tab bar. The accessibility rules from v1 stay. Nothing is applied to the app yet.

Typography: Pretendard

One family for Latin and Hangul (SIL OFL 1.1), self-hosted with dynamic subsets so phones only download the characters a page uses. Titles use weight 800 and slightly tight tracking for a modern feel; body text keeps normal tracking for readability.

Shape, space, elevation

  • Radius: chips 10 · buttons and inputs 14 · icon tiles 14 · cards 20 · sheets 28 · pills full.
  • Spacing: 4px grid. 16px page gutter, 20px card padding, 12px between cards, 32px between sections.
  • Elevation: cards get a barely-there shadow on the grey canvas; sheets and the tab bar float higher. Dark mode drops shadows and uses 1px borders.
  • Icon tiles: 44px rounded squares, soft tint with a strong icon color. Tints are decoration only; meaning always comes from the text label.

Icons: Lucide only

ISC license. 2px stroke on a 24px grid, tree-shaken. Replaces every emoji. Decorative icons are hidden from screen readers; icon-only buttons get a text label.

Components (light | dark)

Special modes

  • Emergency mode (119, the Emergency screen, urgent alerts): plain surface with no tints or shadows, 34px title, 21px text, one 88px Call button placed low for the thumb, no animation, at most three short lines.
  • Show to staff: full screen, pure white (pure black in dark mode), 46px Korean with English underneath, Copy and Close. One shared component for phrases, place names (taxi) and clinic types.
  • Disclaimers: a small grey line with an info icon, placed right after the result or action it relates to. Never collapsed or hidden.

Motion and layout

  • Motion: press-scale 120ms, sheet slide-up 220ms, toast fade 150ms. Nothing loops except the loading shimmer. All of it turns off with reduced motion; emergency screens never animate.
  • Thumb zone: main action buttons stick to the bottom, just above the floating tab bar.
  • Safe areas: top padding uses env(safe-area-inset-top); the tab bar and sheets use env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
  • Targets: 48px minimum, 52px buttons, 56px tabs and checklist rows, 88px Call 119.
  • Large text: everything in rem; layouts reflow to one column at 200% text and the tab bar labels stay visible.

Decisions before I start

  1. Two free dependencies, no API or keys: pretendard (font, SIL OFL 1.1) and lucide-react (icons, ISC). Both allow commercial use.
  2. Primary blue #1B64DA (dark #7DB2FF).
  3. Emoji → Lucide icons across the app.
  4. Safety tab: the scam checker and alert translator become a segmented switch at the top (one shown at a time) instead of two long stacked cards.
  5. Map choices and "Copy Korean name" move into a bottom sheet on trail stops and clinic search.

After approval: (1) tokens and base styles, (2) shared components, (3) Home, Safety, Local, Explore. Tests, typecheck, lint and build after each commit, then the accessibility pass.
